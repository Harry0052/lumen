# Lumen pilot plan

This plan has not been run. No completion rate below is from real people. The counts inside the app are rows in whatever database that copy is using.

## Question

Can someone who is new to a task finish a Lumen guide without a person sitting next to them, and do they come back for a second guide?

The product metric is: of the guide sessions that were started, how many reached the finish screen because the person moved through the steps. A secondary signal is the optional clarity rating.

## Who

10 to 30 adults who have asked a family member for help with email, passwords, or phone updates. Include at least a few people who prefer larger text, and a few who read Spanish. Do not recruit by pretending the guides are official government instructions.

## What they do

1. Open Lumen with no introduction beyond the home page.
2. Start one guide that matches a task they actually have.
3. Stop when they are stuck, when they finish, or after 15 minutes.
4. Say whether they would open it again, in their own words.
5. Optionally leave the clarity rating. Do not require it.

A facilitator may watch. They should not point at the Next button unless the person asks. That ask is a result, not a failure to hide.

## What to record

- Started a guide or left from the home page
- Finished, stopped, or asked for help, and on which step
- Whether the stale-review warning was understood
- Whether Spanish readers hit the English fallback and whether the banner was enough
- Any place someone tried to type a password or a form answer
- Whether they opened a second guide in a later session

Store notes outside the demo database. Do not paste passwords or form answers into Lumen’s feedback box. The box already says not to.

## Decision

- Ship a wider trial only if most starters finish one guide without live help, and at least some return for a second guide.
- Iterate if people finish only when someone points, or if the stale warning and the Spanish fallback are misunderstood.
- Stop the guide format if people cannot finish a four-step demo even with the text in front of them. The brief’s invalidation signal is that outcome: consider a person on a call instead of more automation.

This file is the plan. It is not evidence. The decision today is iterate: the software path exists, and no pilot has been observed.
